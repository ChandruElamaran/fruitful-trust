Who we are

Fruitful Trust began with one small room and a simple belief: nobody should grow up or grow old alone. Today we run a children's home and an elders' home side by side, so the two generations learn from each other every day.

Children read the newspaper aloud to grandparents. Elders tell stories and help with homework. Everything we do is paid for by kind people like you, and every rupee goes to food, health, school fees and shelter.

How we care

For orphan children

  • Safe home, clean beds and three meals a day
  • School admission, books, uniforms and tuition
  • Health check-ups and counselling
  • Sports, music and computer classes

For elders

  • Comfortable rooms with nurse support
  • Nutritious meals and regular medical care
  • Yoga, prayer and storytelling circles
  • Visits from family, friends and volunteers
